The blog ships with built-in SEO so posts are discoverable in search and look right when shared. You control the metadata for each post; the platform handles the sitemap, robots, and structured data.

## Per-post SEO

Every post has an **SEO** tab in the editor with the following fields:

- **Meta title** — the title shown in search results and on social cards. Falls back to the post title if empty.
- **Meta description** — the description shown in search results and on social cards. Falls back to the post excerpt if empty.
- **Meta image** — the image shown on social cards. Choose an upload from the [Media](blog-media) collection. Falls back to the hero image.
- **Preview** — a live preview that shows how the search result and social card will look.
- **Overview** — quick guidance on the recommended length and what's filled in.

### Set SEO for a post

1. Open the post and go to the **SEO** tab.
2. Enter a **Meta title** (aim for 50–60 characters).
3. Enter a **Meta description** (aim for 130–160 characters).
4. Choose a **Meta image**. Use a 1200 × 630 px image for the best result on social platforms.
5. Review the **Preview** before saving.

## Sitemap

The blog automatically publishes a sitemap at `/sitemap.xml`. It includes:

- The home page (`/`).
- The blog index (`/blog`).
- Every published post (`/blog/{slug}`), with `lastmod` based on the post's last update.

You don't need to add posts to the sitemap manually — they appear as soon as they're published.

## Robots

A `robots.txt` is published at `/robots.txt`. It allows crawling of public pages and the blog, and disallows the admin and API:

```text
User-agent: *
Allow: /
Allow: /blog
Disallow: /admin
Disallow: /api
Sitemap: https://anyschool.ai/sitemap.xml
```

## Structured data

Each post page includes JSON-LD structured data for an `Article`, with the post's title, description, hero image, authors, and publish date. This helps search engines render rich results.

You don't need to configure structured data — it's generated for every published post.

## Tips

- Use the Preview on the SEO tab to spot issues before publishing.
- Keep meta titles under 60 characters and descriptions under 160 characters to avoid truncation.
- Upload a dedicated 1200 × 630 px share image for posts you want to promote heavily.
- After publishing, submit your sitemap URL to your search console of choice for faster indexing.
