Categories are reusable labels you attach to blog posts. They help visitors recognise what a post is about on the index and on each post page.

## What this screen is for

- Create and manage the category list used by the [Posts](blog-posts) collection.
- Keep titles and slugs consistent across the public blog.

## What you can see

The Categories list at `/admin/collections/categories` shows:

- Default columns **Title**, **Slug**, and **Updated at**.
- A **Create new** button to add a category.

## Common actions

### Create a category

1. Open the admin and go to **Categories**.
2. Choose **Create new**.
3. Enter a **Title**. The slug is generated for you and can be edited.
4. Save the category. It is now available to assign to posts.

### Edit a category

Open the category, change the title or slug, and save. Existing posts keep their assignment, and the new title appears wherever the category is shown.

### Delete a category

Open the category and delete it. Posts that referenced the category lose that label; their content is unaffected.

## Where categories appear

- **Post editor** — chosen on the **Meta** tab of a post.
- **Blog index** — shown as small badges on each post card.
- **Post page** — shown above the title to set context.

## Permissions

- Anyone signed in to the admin can create, update, and delete categories.
- Public visitors can read categories on the published blog.

## Tips

- Keep category titles short (one or two words) so they fit cleanly on cards.
- Use a small, stable set of categories — too many makes posts harder to scan.
