The Blog is your public-facing content hub on AnySchool.ai. You write, edit, and publish posts in the built-in admin, and visitors read them at `/blog` and `/blog/{slug}`.

## What you can do

- Draft and publish blog posts with rich text, headings, links, images, and uploads.
- Auto-save drafts as you type and schedule posts to publish at a future date.
- Organise posts with categories and assign one or more authors.
- Upload images and reuse them as hero images, in-post media, or social share previews.
- Set per-post SEO metadata (title, description, share image) with a live search and social preview.
- Let visitors browse posts on `/blog`, search by keyword, and read individual posts at `/blog/{slug}`.
- Surface posts to search engines through `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt`, and structured data.

## How it fits together

The blog is powered by Payload CMS and uses four collections:

- **Posts** — the blog articles themselves, including content, hero image, categories, authors, and SEO metadata.
- **Categories** — labels you attach to posts for grouping and display.
- **Media** — uploaded images and files used inside posts.
- **Users** — admin accounts that can sign in and author posts.

You manage all four collections from the admin at `/admin`.

## Tips

- Write clear, recognisable titles; they appear on the blog index, in search results, and in social previews.
- Add a short excerpt to every post — it shows on the index page and is used as fallback text for SEO.
- Set a hero image so posts look complete on the index, on social shares, and at the top of the post page.
